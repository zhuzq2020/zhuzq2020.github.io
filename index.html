About me

I am Zaki, a robotics software engineer working on the systems that connect robot motion to real hardware.

My work focuses on EtherCAT communication, servo integration, real-time Linux, and multi-joint robot control. I build tools for diagnosing drives, inspecting timing behavior, and bringing motion-control software onto physical robots.

This website brings together my engineering projects, experiments, and technical notes. I am particularly interested in reliable hardware interfaces, robot teleoperation, and the relationship between control algorithms and measured system behavior.
